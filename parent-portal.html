What this account cannot reach

A parent account can open its own children and nothing else. There is no school roll, no class register, and no way to look up another family — not by name, and not by trying a different id in the address bar.

Asking for another family's child returns 404 Student not found — the same answer as asking for a child who does not exist. A parent trying ids in turn learns nothing about who else attends the school.
