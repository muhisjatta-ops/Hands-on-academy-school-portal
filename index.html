Why Hands-On Academy

What makes the school what it is

The name is the method. These are the things we would want a parent to ask us about on a tour.

Practical and laboratory work

Pupils handle the equipment, not just read about it

Laboratory science begins in Grade 4 and practical subjects run through upper basic, up to external examination in Grade 9.

That is also why the bill carries a laboratory fee from Grade 4 and a practical fee from Grade 8. Those charges pay for materials that get used rather than displayed — slides, reagents, and the equipment in the photograph.

Abacus from Grade 1

Mental arithmetic taught on the abacus in every lower basic class, building number sense before calculators enter the picture.

A class teacher who knows your child

Every class has a teacher who takes the register each morning and can tell you how your child is doing without checking a file.

Nine levels on one campus

Daycare through Grade 9. A child can spend thirteen years here and never change school, with their records following them throughout.

Parents who can see in

Attendance, marks, report cards and fee balances live in the school portal — not in a drawer in the office.

A safe, orderly school

Registers are taken daily and absences followed up the same day, because a child missing school is the first thing a parent should hear about.

Admissions

How to apply

Bring your child's birth certificate, and their last report card if they are transferring from another school.

  1. Visit the school

    Talk to the office about the right level for your child's age and previous schooling.

  2. Register

    The registration fee is paid once, at entry. Your child is issued a student ID that stays with them through the school.

  3. Settle the first term

    First-term fees include uniform, and in the basic grades the year's book bill.

  4. Start

    Attendance, marks and fee balances are recorded in the portal from the first day, where you can see them.

One thing to know: Level One, Grade One and Grade Seven each begin a stage, so every pupil in them registers afresh — including children moving up from the level below. Fees are non-refundable.

Fees 2026/2027

What it costs

Fees are charged per term. The first term is higher for new and re-registering pupils, because registration and uniform are paid once at entry. The accounts office will give you the full schedule for your child's level, and fees are non-refundable.

Where to pay

Payments go directly into the school's accounts. Bring the deposit slip to the accounts office and a receipt will be issued against your child's record.
