Three figures need confirming before any parent is quoted

Every line item on the printed bill was added up. Six of the nine levels reconcile exactly. Three do not, and the differences are shown in red in the tables below.

  • Grade 7 year total — the three term figures sum to 35,000, but the bill states 33,500. The term rows look internally consistent, so the year total appears to be the slip.
  • Grade 8 Term 1 (new) — items give 17,000; the bill says 16,500. Here the bill contradicts itself: its own returning-student figure of 10,500 equals 17,000 less registration, uniform and book bill. The missing 500 looks like the exam fee.
  • Grade 9 Terms 2 and 3 — seven items sum to 10,500, but 11,000 is stated, and the year total is built on 11,000. Either an item is missing from the table or the total is 500 high. Also worth confirming: Grade 9 Term 1 shows portal and lab fees at 250 rather than the 500 used everywhere else.

The portal charges the line items, not the stated totals — an invoice has to list what it charges row by row. So Grade 7 currently bills 35,000 for the year.
